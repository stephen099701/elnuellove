FEATURED BOOK · THE CHOICE

It began with
a dream.

46 pages · 15 chapters · a prayer and a personal declaration.

In a quiet forest, a man is asked to choose between two bows. One has white edges. The other is black.

That dream became the starting point for The Choice: a personal, Bible-centered exploration of the decisions that shape our lives. From competing voices to everyday habits, Nuel Love invites readers to consider what it means to choose carefully and live with faith.
